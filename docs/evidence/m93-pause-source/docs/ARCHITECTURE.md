# Current vertical-slice architecture

`scenes/main.tscn` has one composition root, `scripts/main.gd`. It creates the systems and injects references; there are no autoloads or hard-coded node paths. Gameplay verification uses that same scene.

Collision layers: world 1, player 2, hostile 4, bike 8, cart 16 plus world bit, worker 32. World obstacles use solid BoxShape3D where box overlap semantics matter. Terrain uses concave mesh collision. Decorative foliage has no collision.

## State and transaction rules

- Pause gates movement, combat, riding, cargo, resources and workers. Mounting hides/disables the walking actor and gives vehicle controls to the seat. Dismount checks ground, slope, elevation, line of sight and capsule clearance; airborne or fast dismount is rejected.
- Flight folding is rejected in midair. Deployment needs clear space at both start and completion; obstruction during deployment folds safely. AircraftTailCollision covers the rear shaft, fin, stabilizer and conservative propeller sweep during transitions and flight; its shapes also participate in physical movement and heading clearance. Full wing collision follows visual attitude; two transition colliders follow each panel while it unfolds/folds. Proposed progress and visual attitude reject world intersections; a blocked arc waits with HUD guidance and can be cleared by driving away. A landing needs actual walkable support beneath a chassis axle, so a wing-only floor contact cannot enable folding or saving. A cart must be detached before deployment.
- Inventories expose independent snapshots. Failed transfers leave both inventories unchanged. Full destinations cannot consume source stock. Parcels cannot be sold as ordinary commodities. Contracts set intermediate states before inventory signals to reject reentrant acceptance/delivery.
- Worker jobs keep the acquired/delivered stage in JobExecutor. Retrying a blocked destination must not reacquire source stock. Recall returns carried goods to home storage. Failed workers retain cargo and expose their reason instead of silently dropping items.
- Stopping a routine leaves the active job intact. Its successful transaction advances the phase/cycle exactly once, even after stop/save/load; stopping prevents future automatic assignment. The schema checks saved routine membership against the executor phase.

## Scaling and persistence boundaries

Generated outer chunks are freed when retired. Authored core meshes are hidden and collision disabled at distance, while logical stock/job nodes remain resident. Workers switch to 1 Hz abstract movement and transaction execution; returning restores visible physics. Native and Web continuous-flight verifiers crossed 23.95 km with peak 25 outer chunks. This proves bounded traversal, not completed regional content.

Stock revision 2 extends save version 1 with independent regional resource depletion. Missing stock revision means legacy revision 1; only newly introduced sources initialize from configured stock, while malformed current data is rejected.

Save version 1 captures grounded/stopped player and vehicles, inventories/stocks, economy, survey flags, hostile health and each registered worker’s job stage, regional discoveries, regional encounter records, delivery route and each worker’s routine phase. Validation rejects malformed records before mutation. Browser persistence has a separate fresh-page reload verifier. Flight saves are not supported. Roster revision 2 adds independent worker records while migrating earlier Mara-only saves.

## Known limitations

Arcade bike/aircraft/trailer physics; no suspension or realistic aerodynamics. Geometric animation and placeholder models/sounds. Navigation is a coarse local grid, not full-world pathfinding. One authored village and a small catalog. Repeat courier contracts and market-ore ammunition supplies support continued play. No worker wages, touch/controller support or low-end hardware validation. The measured village rendering budget is 354 native draw calls in a pinned village view on one M4 Pro; hidden original meshes remain allocated after batching. Some verifier fixtures directly establish poses; passing them is not exhaustive human playtesting.


WorkerManager registers Mara and Ivo with stable IDs and separate WorkerRoutineState instances. N opens orders; Tab selects a worker, with combat blocked while the panel is open. Each actor retains its own executor, inventory, navigation path and routine counters. Shared warehouse transfers remain atomic through Inventory. WorkerRosterState serializes and validates the roster before restore: the original `worker` field retains Mara for legacy saves, while roster revision 2 requires the exact registered `other_workers` records keyed by ID. Revision 1 initializes only newly introduced Ivo; unknown, missing, duplicated or malformed current records reject the whole restore. Original single-worker routine properties still address Mara for older integrations.


QuarryAdit adds a short above-ground rock gallery to Red Mesa's owner chunk. Side, back and roof boxes provide solid collision while the existing terrain supplies its floor; this is a small authored chamber, not terrain excavation or a general cave generator. ResourceSource red_mesa_ore stays logically resident while its presentation follows player proximity. Stock revision 3 initializes only sources introduced after a loaded file's revision; current-revision missing stocks remain invalid. The quarry endpoint verifier isolates guards after arrival, while the separate encounter verifier exercises real regional combat.

RegionalBrook defines one bounded channel, shared water resource and chunk-owned bridge. CoastalRegion and WorldBackdrop apply matching channel and end-cap parameters; RegionScenery excludes submerged foliage and clears the crossing approaches. The bridge has a continuous collision deck and solid barriers, while water uses WaterSafety without per-frame Area3D overlap nodes. WorldManager retains one visibility-limited WaterSurface. Saved deck positions are retained while SaveSystem rebuilds the full desired terrain window before resuming simulation. This is an authored crossing rather than a connected river network.

CoastalRegion generates closed clockwise limestone meshes for matching exterior lighting and collision; the same helper covers arch undersides. CliffGrove uses a separate seed stream and static mesh batching for small trees above the rocks. Preserving the original rock random-number calls keeps subsequent authored collision placement unchanged.

VehicleHUD computes course and signed heading error from actual bike position/yaw and a supplied target. GameHUD selects an explicit map waypoint first, then an active delivery destination. The new heading/vertical-speed line reads the controller without changing movement. Nearby wording indicates proximity only; logistics retains delivery validation and payment ownership.

IncomingDamageFeedback listens to accepted HealthComponent damage and restoration signals. It draws only peripheral strips, ignores input/focus, and clears when Main pauses simulation. GameAudio routes the new original hurt clip through the existing eight-voice pool. Rejected damage emits no feedback; no combat state, damage amount or invulnerability rule is changed.


WaterSurface renders all three WaterBody resources with the same world-coordinate shader. Its animation uniform advances independently of gameplay physics; the water-camera verifier freezes only that uniform while moving its camera. Safety remains an independent WaterBody depth query.

CargoCart owns its initial home pose and a recover() endpoint with a recovered signal. Detached bounds/fall recovery resets the cart there; attached recovery delegates to BikeController so existing seat/hitch listeners move the complete assembly. WorldManager refreshes tiers synchronously on cart recovery, suspending distant home cargo before unloaded ground can affect it. WaterSafety uses that endpoint for submerged detached cargo as well. The fall cutoff is -64 m, below legitimate seabed terrain.

Core and streamed ground share the continuous terrain surface shader, with a bounded coastal tint; terrain geometry and collision remain unchanged. LogisticsWorld selects only a nearby, reachable source or station for its action label and refreshes that selection before U transactions.

Village and streamed shelter roof meshes carry triangle collision; Snowwatch’s top cap uses its matching box shape. Regional collision belongs to the same retiring terrain chunk as its presentation. BikeController uses a 2 cm safe margin to recover ground contacts at mesh seams; varied-direction, loaded-towing and actual-wall fixtures cover the corrected route. Shapes and collision masks are unchanged.

PlayerVisual separates thighs and lower legs into independently batched animated joints. Local sole-height adjustment keeps the procedural gait near its standing foot plane; this does not query terrain or implement foot IK. BikeVisual calls the shared riding pose. Player collision, weapon anchors and worker navigation are independent of these presentation joints.

RegionalSurveySite provides four resident, proximity-hidden boards separate from Main.sites, which remains the original three-stamp route. Main gives InteractionController a separate combined array. Regional boards require stopped grounded living on-foot access in the READY combat state; the controller retains line-of-sight validation. Board color and prompt derive from PlayerController.spawn_position, already validated and persisted by SaveSystem. WorldStreamer.prepare_focus_collision synchronously retires stale chunks and builds the checkpoint center on player recovery; the rest of the bounded window uses normal incremental streaming. Vehicle home recovery retains its existing behavior.

FieldMap can track the checkpoint value separately from actor references. Refreshing reads PlayerController.spawn_position and chooses local/world scope only when that value changes. Equipment, workers, ordinary clicks and Clear leave checkpoint mode explicitly. Save restoration changes the existing checkpoint vector, so active session guidance follows it without adding serialized map state. The Checkpoint control shares the existing equipment row.

CoastalRegion limits fog influence on its procedural sky to 0.25, retaining the authored blue-to-warm gradient while preserving terrain fog density and lighting.

Authored building roofs share a world-coordinate terracotta shader with derivative-based detail fading and a normal-based mask that leaves vertical gables plain. StaticBatcher accepts ShaderMaterial only when the mesh explicitly declares batchable_world_shader; the roof opts in because its shading does not depend on per-building local coordinates. Standard-material grouping remains unchanged. Eaves, frames and shutters share cached existing materials, with no new collision or random calls.

CargoCart animates tire rotation from signed displacement projected onto its forward direction along the actual floor plane. Ground contact and a small horizontal-motion threshold reject vertical settling; the original total 3D travel statistic and towing physics remain unchanged.

CartCanopy defines a fixed elliptical cross-section used by both the double-sided canvas mesh and a convex upper collision shape. CargoCart retains its lower chassis box and synchronizes the upper shape to the ground-aligned visual transform; an intersecting proposed tilt retains the previous pose. The upper shape participates in existing translation and yaw checks. VehicleClearance computes convex support from transformed points as well as box support, preserving bounded slope adjustment. Cargo capacities, mass tuning, inventory APIs and save fields are unchanged.

RiderCollision builds a cleaned convex shape from the posed rider’s visible mesh faces in BikeVisual coordinates, removing duplicate/interior vertices. VehicleSeat gates mounting on clear space, toggles the shape with ownership and restores that state from existing mounted save data. BikeController checks proposed rider attitude before wing synchronization and then synchronizes the rider shape to the final visual transform, preventing divergent wing/rider poses. Only mounted bikes carry the rider shape; on-foot PlayerController collision is unchanged.

WindAmbience is one non-positional AudioStreamPlayer owned by GameAudio. A 12-second original filtered-noise WAV loops without runtime synthesis or chunk-owned voices. At most one upward world ray every 0.25 seconds controls shelter attenuation; terrain-relative altitude adjusts exposed gain from -24 to -18 dB. Gain approaches its target smoothly; disabled/pause stops and resets it immediately. Existing eight effect voices and the engine loop remain separate. The generator only writes wind.wav.

AudioPreferences applies bounded master-bus volume/mute and atomically persists user://audio_settings.cfg independently of journey saves. AudioSettingsPanel owns a 0.3-second debounce timer and emits a write failure for HUD feedback. Main injects the same preferences object into the HUD. Verification uses separate probe paths and restores the initial master settings; it never writes the player’s preferences file. Wind uses explicit stream playback so the mixer owns continuous looping on both targets.

RegionalTreeMesh appends forked stems and clustered crowns into RegionScenery's existing shared broadleaf ArrayMesh. Population seeds, transform generation, biome density, clearings and physics remain owned by RegionScenery. No per-tree scene nodes, colliders or processing are added; each chunk keeps one foliage MultiMesh per kind. The regional verifier includes a rendered forest frame-budget sample in addition to deterministic retirement/recreation checks.

WorkerActor's visible PerformTask pose turns toward its generic JobDefinition.source when that node still exists and lifts the right hand forward. The presentation guard tolerates a removed source after goods were acquired; it does not alter JobExecutor transactions, navigation, work duration or FSM transitions. Abstract workers still skip visual updates.

PlayerCombat uses world+vehicle cover for melee/lock sight and muzzle obstruction, adding hostile bodies for its camera shot query. DamageSystem.clear_line accepts an optional collision mask and preserves its world-only default for interaction and existing AI callers. No vehicle damage or bullet penetration system is implied. The vehicle_cover suite proves actual aircraft/chassis obstruction with clear-shot, shoulder-camera, target-lock and melee controls.

Hostile sight and committed strikes use DamageSystem.can_see_health with the same world+vehicle combat cover mask. HealthComponent carries transient aim-anchor and collision-receiver references; VehicleSeat binds these to an upper-chest marker and the mounted rider shape, then clears/rebinds them on exit and journey restoration. Only a ray terminating on that specific rider shape counts as exposed mounted visibility; chassis, tail and world hits remain cover. Save payloads are unchanged. NPC physical movement/navigation around vehicles is still separate unfinished work.
